02How the customer sees us

One contract, one counterparty, one party accountable.

  • Elishah signs every contract. The customer never sees three freelancers working together.
  • Partners are introduced by title: Elishah Commercial Partner, Elishah Product Partner.
  • Elishah owns the IP and licenses it into each deployment. The IP is never co-owned deal by deal.
  • Partners share in the profit of the deals they create, shape and support.

Later we can add a European OpCo or JV (e.g. Elishah Europe B.V.). It would license IP from Elishah and change nothing about how partners are paid.

04IP in every contract

Four IP buckets, written into every MSA.

A · Background IP

100% Elishah

Everything Elishah owned before the engagement.

B · Platform improvements

100% Elishah

Generic work built during a project: retrieval, connectors, model router, workflow. This applies even when the customer paid for it. They get a licence.

C · Customer-specific

Negotiable

Their configuration, workflows, prompts and business rules. Can be licensed exclusively or assigned to them. Elishah keeps the underlying building blocks.

D · Customer data

100% Customer

Documents, embeddings, logs, operational data. No ambiguity, and this is central to how we sell sovereignty.

"We must own it"

We don't lose the deal over this. The customer owns the bespoke deliverables, for example a MaintenanceWorkflow. Elishah keeps the WorkflowEngine, DocumentParser, ModelRouter and SAPConnector. Sophisticated buyers are used to this arrangement.

04Compliance from day one

Every euro can be traced to documented commercial work.

Success fees paid to intermediaries get scrutiny from auditors, acquirers and regulators across Europe. Good hygiene here protects the partner as much as it protects Elishah.

KYC & identity

Identity and beneficial-owner disclosure for every partner entity.

Conflicts & officials

Disclosure of any conflicts and of relationships with public officials.

Anti-bribery undertaking

Signed in the MCA and renewed annually.

Documented scope

A written description of the services behind every point earned.

Clean payments

Paid only to the contracted entity and its account, against invoice, with no cash or third-party payments.

Records

Audit trail kept to UK Bribery Act, Sapin II and EU AML standards.
